This guide takes you from an empty Forms tab to a live submission in your inbox.

## Create a website form

Use this path when you already have a form on your own site.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Forms tab">
    Sign in to the [SwiftAgent dashboard](https://swiftagents.org), go to
    **Ticketing**, and select the **Forms** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a website form">
    Choose **Website form**. You are asked for two things:

    - **Website link**: the site you are installing on, such as
      `https://example.com`. It must be a full `http://` or `https://` URL.
    - **Alert email**: where SwiftAgent emails you when a submission arrives.

    Click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy your keys and snippet">
    The second step shows everything you need to install:

    | Value | Looks like | What it is for |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Snippet | Two `<script>` tags | Paste this straight into your site |
    | Public key | `SDPK-272…` | Identifies the form from the browser |
    | API key | A secret string | Server-side calls only, keep it private |
    | Form ID | A UUID | Referencing the form in the API |

    Copy the snippet now. Click **Save and Exit** when you are done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The full **API key** and **public key** are shown only once, on this screen and
  when you regenerate them. Store them somewhere safe before you leave the
  drawer. Afterwards the dashboard shows only the key prefixes.
</Warning>

## Install the snippet

Paste the snippet before the closing `</body>` tag on every page that has a form
you want to capture:

```html
<script src="https://api.swiftagents.org/api/v1/public/forms/widget.js"></script>
<script>
  SwiftForms.init({ publicKey: "SDPK-272XXXXXXXXXXXX" });
</script>
```

Replace the public key with your own. That is the whole installation. Every
`<form>` on the page is bound automatically as soon as the DOM is ready.

<Note>
  Prefer the snippet shown in your dashboard over the one above. The script
  works out where to send submissions by reading the `src` of the tag that
  loaded it, so the host in your snippet is the host that receives your data.
</Note>

## Verify it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit your own form">
    Open the page in a browser and send a real submission with recognisable
    values, such as the name `Test Submission`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the network tab">
    In your browser devtools, look for a `POST` to
    `/api/v1/public/forms/widget/submit`. Seeing the request confirms the script
    is bound to your form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the inbox">
    Back in **Ticketing → Forms**, your submission appears under the website,
    then the page path, then the form. Unread submissions are listed first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If nothing arrives, work through [troubleshooting](/forms/troubleshooting).

## Create an online form

Use this path when you want a form hosted by SwiftAgent instead of one on your
own site.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add an online form">
    In the **Forms** tab, choose **Online form**. You are asked for:

    - **Form image**: a URL to the image shown with the form.
    - **Form title**: the name you will recognise it by, such as
      `NG Ballerz Form`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the form URL">
    The second step adds a **form URL** to the usual keys and snippet. That URL
    is the form itself, ready to share in an email, a post, or a link on your
    site. No installation is required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Online forms are the only kind you can reply to from the inbox. If answering
  submitters by email matters to you, prefer an online form.
</Tip>

## Control which forms are captured

By default the script binds to every form on the page. Two attributes let you
change that per form.

Exclude a form entirely:

```html
<form data-swift-ignore>
  <input name="q" />
</form>
```

Give a form a readable name in the inbox:

```html
<form data-swift-name="Enterprise quote request">
  <input name="company" />
</form>
```

Without `data-swift-name`, the script derives a name from the form's `title`,
`aria-label`, or nearest heading. See
[field capture](/forms/field-capture#how-forms-are-named) for the full order.
