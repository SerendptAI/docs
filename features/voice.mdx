---
title: "Voice Input"
description: "Let visitors speak their message instead of typing."
---

The widget supports **speech-to-text** input. Visitors can dictate a message and
have it transcribed directly into the chat input, which is handy on mobile or for
longer questions.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start listening">
    The visitor activates the microphone. The widget requests microphone
    permission from the browser on first use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Voice activity detection">
    A lightweight VAD (voice-activity detector) watches the audio stream and only
    records while the visitor is actually speaking, ignoring brief noise blips.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Transcription">
    When the visitor pauses, the captured audio is sent to the SwiftAgent voice
    endpoint and the returned transcript is dropped into the chat input, ready to
    review and send.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Requirements

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Microphone permission" icon="microphone">
    The browser prompts for mic access on first use. If denied, voice input is
    unavailable until the visitor re-grants it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Secure context" icon="lock">
    Browsers only expose the microphone over **HTTPS** (or `localhost`). On plain
    HTTP, voice input is disabled by the browser.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Voice input transcribes speech into text; the conversation itself still happens
  as chat. The visitor can always edit the transcript before sending.
</Note>

## Tuning

Voice activity detection is calibrated for typical speech: it starts recording
after a short burst of sound and stops after a brief silence, with a minimum
recording length to filter out incidental noise. These defaults work across most
environments without configuration.
