title: "Stroll (Agent 047)"
description: "Teach the widget's agent your product, so it can give visitors screenshot-backed directions to any feature."
---

**Stroll** is the crawler behind **Agent 047**. Give it a sandbox account and the
URL of your logged-in app, and it signs in, walks through your authenticated
pages, and uses AI vision to understand what's on each one. The result is a
navigation map the chat agent draws on to answer "how do I find X?" with a real,
[annotated screenshot](/features/chat#guided-navigation) instead of a guess.

Stroll can re-run on a schedule to keep that map current, or on demand whenever
you've shipped changes worth re-crawling.

<Note>
  Guided navigation answers become available automatically as soon as a Stroll
  run completes successfully, there's nothing else to turn on.
</Note>

## Prerequisites

Before you start, create a **dedicated sandbox account** in your own product:
a login Stroll can use that isn't tied to a real customer or teammate. Stroll
will be driving a real browser through your authenticated pages, so give it an
account you're comfortable automating.

## Configure Stroll

Stroll settings live in two equivalent places, edit either one and the change
shows up immediately in the other:

- **Settings → Stroll** in the dashboard sidebar
- The **Sandbox** section inside the widget's own settings drawer
  (**Dashboard → Overview** → your widget card → **Settings**)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Stroll settings">
    Sign in to the [SwiftAgent dashboard](https://swiftagents.org) and go to
    **Settings → Stroll** (or open your widget's settings drawer and expand
    **Sandbox**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Dashboard URL">
    Enter the **Dashboard URL**: the logged-in page you want Agent 047 to
    inspect after it signs in, for example `https://app.example.com/home`.
    This field is required, Stroll has nothing to crawl without it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a Login URL (optional)">
    If your app has a dedicated sign-in page, enter it as the **Login URL**.
    This is where Agent 047 will enter the sandbox credentials before heading
    to the Dashboard URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add sandbox credentials">
    Enter the **Email/Username** and **Password** for the sandbox account.
    Both are required.

    <Warning>
      Always use a dedicated sandbox account here, never a real customer's
      login. Stroll signs in and clicks around your product automatically;
      treat the credentials the same way you would any other automated
      integration.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave Sandbox mode on">
    **Sandbox mode** is checked by default and should stay that way. Only turn
    it off if you specifically intend to point Stroll at production.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save your changes. Stroll now has what it needs to run on its next
    scheduled pass, or immediately via [Stroll Now](#run-stroll-on-demand).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Advanced options

Advanced options are collapsed by default. Most teams never need to touch them.

<ParamField path="Schedule" type="cron expression" default="0 2 * * * (daily, 2 AM)">
  How often Stroll automatically re-crawls your product. The dashboard offers
  presets, hourly, every 6 hours, every 12 hours, daily at midnight, daily at
  2 AM, daily at 9 AM, weekly on Sunday at midnight, and monthly on the 1st,
  or you can enter a custom cron expression.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Max pages" type="number" default="50">
  The crawl limit for a single Stroll run. Stroll follows links breadth-first
  from your Dashboard URL and stops once it hits this page count.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Pre-auth URL" type="string">
  An optional URL to visit before the crawl starts, for apps that don't use a
  plain login form. Use this for products that sign the sandbox account in via
  an auto-login or token link rather than a username/password form.
</ParamField>

## Run Stroll on demand

Click **Stroll Now** to trigger an out-of-schedule run right away, useful after
you've shipped a change worth re-crawling. The button disables for 30 seconds
after each use to prevent accidental repeat runs.

## Plan limits

Stroll runs are metered by your billing plan rather than gated behind a
feature flag: every plan includes a monthly allotment of strolls, and higher
tiers include more. If you're on a plan nearing or at its limit, the dashboard
shows a usage banner with an option to upgrade. Check your plan's limits page
in the dashboard for the number that applies to your account.
