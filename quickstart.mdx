This guide gets a working chat widget onto your page with a single script tag.

## Prerequisites

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your Company ID">
    Sign in to the [SwiftAgent dashboard](https://swiftagents.org) and copy your
    **Company ID**: a UUID that looks like `2883e40b-4ad9-4352-b78e-8b2053e847e3`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your API key">
    Copy your **API key** (prefixed `swa_live_…`) from the same dashboard. The
    widget needs it to authenticate every chat and upload request.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add the script

Paste this just before the closing `</body>` tag of any page. Both
`data-company-id` and `data-api-key` are required:

```html
<script
  src="https://widget.swiftagents.org/dist/widget-ui.js"
  data-company-id="YOUR_COMPANY_ID"
  data-api-key="swa_live_xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx"
  defer
></script>
```

That's it. The widget **auto-mounts** as soon as the page finishes loading and a
floating launcher appears in the bottom-right corner.

<Note>
  Replace `https://widget.swiftagents.org/dist/widget-ui.js` with the script URL shown in
  your dashboard, `YOUR_COMPANY_ID` with your own Company ID, and the
  `data-api-key` value with your own key.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Treat the API key as public; it ships in the browser. Scope it to the widget's
  chat endpoints only, and never reuse a server-side secret key here.
</Warning>

## Verify it's working

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reload your page">
    Open your site in a browser. The launcher should appear within a second.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the chat">
    Click the launcher. On first open you'll see a brief loading skeleton, then
    a greeting from your agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a message">
    Type a question and press Enter. The response streams in live.
  </Step>
</Steps>
