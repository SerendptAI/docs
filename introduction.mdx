The **SwiftAgent Widget** is a self-contained, embeddable AI chat agent that drops
into any website with one `<script>` tag. It answers visitor questions, guides
users through your product with annotated screenshots, creates support tickets,
and hands off to a human agent when needed, all without touching your existing
front-end code.

The widget is built as a standalone IIFE bundle with **Shadow DOM style isolation**,
so it never inherits or leaks CSS from the host page. It works on plain HTML sites,
server-rendered apps, and single-page applications alike.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the script">
    Drop the `<script>` tag on your page with your `data-company-id` and
    `data-api-key`. The widget auto-mounts once the DOM is ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Visitor opens the chat">
    A floating launcher appears in the corner (or you bind your own button in
    [button mode](/configuration/display-modes)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="The agent responds">
    Messages stream to the SwiftAgent backend, which replies with AI-generated
    answers, navigation guides, or a ticket, reconnecting over a realtime
    socket for any human follow-up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You need both a **Company ID** and an **API key** from your
  [SwiftAgent dashboard](https://swiftagents.org) before you can embed the widget.
</Note>
